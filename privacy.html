Privacy policy

This tool is for its owner's private use. Google Drive data is used only to save and restore the owner's monitoring files and to verify their integrity.

Access and storage

The tool requests only drive.file. It does not request access to the owner's entire Drive. It accesses files it creates and files or folders the owner explicitly selects. The durable monitoring files are stored in the owner's Google Drive and are not published on this site.

Use and sharing

Google user data is not sold, disclosed to other people, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The tool has no analytics. Processing infrastructure acts only on the owner's instructions; no additional third-party use of Drive data is authorized.

Control and deletion

The owner can delete stored files directly in Google Drive and revoke the tool's permission in Google Account settings. Revocation stops further authorized access but does not automatically delete existing files.

This website

These static pages collect no forms, cookies or analytics of their own. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process normal request information under GitHub's own privacy policy. No Drive data or credentials are sent to this website.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
